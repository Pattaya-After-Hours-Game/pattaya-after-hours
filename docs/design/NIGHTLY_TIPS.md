# Nightly tips and stockout recovery

The reported failure is an open Blue Hour with ฿39 and no stock; a normal
six-pack costs ฿84. Nightly tips improve tomorrow's float, while a visible
recovery action must solve today's lack of stock.

## Rules

- Bank ฿10 per customer visit on its first successful drink sale. A second/third
  drink adds no extra tip. Arrivals, pool-only guests and failed serves add none.
- This is a house tip-jar bonus earned through actual service, additional to the
  existing drink budget. It is not a staff wage deduction or a drink commission.
- Count all venue jars once at nightly street closure, before clean-up, including
  an explicit overnight skip. Daytime toggles, short shift reports and reloads
  never pay tips. Last-venue late trading delays the natural settlement.
- Credit cash, weekly revenue and total street earnings; show a separate report.
  Exclude tips from drink/pool counters, healthy-service margins, party/promotion
  attribution and the five-second trading-rate sample. No offline tip projection.
- Save pending served visits, cumulative paid tips, the last report and the settled
  night. Older campaigns begin at zero; existing guests with prior drink sales
  cannot retroactively earn a tip on their next round.
- Put Bo's recovery delivery beside the stock warning: zero stock, insufficient
  cash for the current pack, legal opening and no active recovery period.
  Supply six consignment drinks, charge ฿14 on each sale, put staff off duty and
  cover overhead for two minutes. Existing orders and owner cooldowns remain.
  Do not change normal six/twelve-unit delivery rules or grant cash.
